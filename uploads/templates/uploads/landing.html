The Problem

Most founders ship without ever testing usability

Early-stage products rarely get structured UX review before launch. Founders validate the idea, build the MVP, and ship — assuming users will figure it out. Most don't.

Silent

Churn happens before analytics can flag a pattern. Users encounter friction, say nothing, and leave. You find out weeks later.

Early

Your first cohort forms an opinion in minutes. Preventable UX failures in that window compound into retention problems that are expensive to reverse.

Costly

Rebuilding flows post-launch burns engineering time you don't have. Structured UX review before ship is faster and cheaper than fixing it after.

The problem isn't that founders don't care about UX. It's that structured usability testing requires researchers, participants, and time that pre-seed teams don't have. Blinkd closes that gap.

The Solution

Upload your flow. Simulate your user. Fix before launch.

Blinkd uses AI to simulate specific user personas navigating your product — mapping every friction point to documented UX research and generating ranked, actionable fixes.

AI-Powered User Flow Simulation

Simulates step-by-step interaction through your product using psychologically accurate personas — executives, first-timers, seniors, analysts, and more.

Friction and Cognitive Overload Detection

Identifies where users hesitate, get confused, or drop off — mapping every issue to a validated UX pattern with a severity rating.

Prioritized, Actionable Recommendations

Structured recommendations ranked by impact on your stated user goal — not vague suggestions, but specific fixes with clear rationale and expected outcome.
