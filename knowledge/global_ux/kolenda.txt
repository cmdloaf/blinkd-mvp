TITLE: Global Bad UX Knowledge Base (Kolenda.io)
SOURCE: Nick Kolenda — UX Guidelines (kolenda.io)

INFERENCE RULE:
When a BAD UX pattern is detected, infer a GOOD UX improvement that:
- reduces cognitive load
- improves clarity toward the stated user goal
- aligns with the selected persona's expectations
- avoids prescribing UI styling or visual aesthetics
- remains actionable and context-aware

---

ID: kolenda_choice_overload
Category: Cognitive Load

Description:
Interfaces that present too many options simultaneously overwhelm users and slow decision-making.

Symptoms:
- Long ungrouped menus
- Multiple equally weighted actions
- Feature lists without prioritization

Impact:
Increases mental effort and hesitation.

---

ID: kolenda_no_context
Category: Cognitive Load

Description:
Presenting raw numbers or data without relative meaning forces users to perform mental calculations.

Symptoms:
- Exact timestamps instead of relative time
- Metrics without benchmarks
- Numbers without explanation

Impact:
Slows comprehension and increases cognitive load.

---

ID: kolenda_no_feedback
Category: Trust

Description:
Users receive little or no indication that an action is being processed or has succeeded.

Symptoms:
- Silent submissions
- No loading or success indicators
- Delayed system response without reassurance

Impact:
Creates uncertainty and mistrust.

---

ID: kolenda_no_input_tolerance
Category: Error Handling

Description:
Interfaces reject common human errors instead of accommodating them.

Symptoms:
- Strict input formats without guidance
- Error messages without recovery steps
- No tolerance for typos or mistakes

Impact:
Users feel blamed and frustrated.

---

ID: kolenda_goal_misalignment
Category: Flow Integrity

Description:
The system enforces rigid flows that conflict with how users want to complete tasks.

Symptoms:
- No shortcuts or customization
- Forced linear paths
- Interruptions that block task completion

Impact:
Creates friction and resistance.

---

ID: kolenda_assumed_knowledge
Category: Onboarding

Description:
Interfaces rely on jargon or concepts unfamiliar to novice users.

Symptoms:
- Technical terminology without explanation
- No contextual help
- Expert-only language

Impact:
Excludes and intimidates new users.

---

ID: kolenda_hard_to_interact
Category: Interaction Design

Description:
Interactive elements are small, cramped, or difficult to reach.

Symptoms:
- Tiny buttons or icons
- Narrow hover or click zones
- Controls that disappear too quickly

Impact:
Increases physical effort and misclicks.

---

ID: kolenda_accusatory_errors
Category: Error Handling

Description:
Error feedback does not help users understand or fix the problem.

Symptoms:
- Generic error messages
- Blaming language
- No suggested next step

Impact:
Users feel stuck and discouraged.

---

ID: kolenda_no_safe_exit
Category: Trust

Description:
Actions feel irreversible, increasing anxiety and hesitation.

Symptoms:
- Permanent changes without undo
- Excessive confirmation dialogs
- No history or rollback

Impact:
Users act cautiously or avoid action.

---

ID: kolenda_unpredictable_outcomes
Category: Usability

Description:
Users cannot easily anticipate what will happen after an interaction.

Symptoms:
- Vague labels
- Unexpected results
- Actions that trigger unrelated changes

Impact:
Breaks trust and predictability.

---

ID: kolenda_ignores_skimming
Category: Cognitive Load

Description:
Interfaces require deep reading to understand key information.

Symptoms:
- Dense paragraphs
- Buried important details
- No hierarchy or emphasis

Impact:
Users miss critical information.

---

ID: kolenda_poor_location_awareness
Category: Navigation

Description:
Users cannot tell where they are in a process or system.

Symptoms:
- Missing breadcrumbs
- No progress indicators
- Unclear page purpose

Impact:
Creates disorientation and hesitation.

---

ID: kolenda_hidden_interactivity
Category: Interaction Design

Description:
Users cannot tell which elements are interactive.

Symptoms:
- Links that look like text
- Buttons that resemble static elements
- No hover or focus feedback

Impact:
Reduces engagement and discoverability.

---

ID: kolenda_visual_noise
Category: Visual Design

Description:
Too many elements compete for attention at once.

Symptoms:
- Decorative content overshadowing actions
- Redundant instructions
- Crowded layouts

Impact:
Fragments attention and slows task completion.
