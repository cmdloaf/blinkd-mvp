ID: cta_misplacement
Category: Conversion Clarity

Bad UX Pattern:
Primary call-to-action is positioned far from the context that motivates it. The action button is spatially disconnected from the information that builds user intent. Checkout buttons placed far from basket summaries, sign-up CTAs below the fold, or action buttons separated from explanatory content by unrelated elements.

Symptoms:
- Users complete mental commitment but cannot find the action button
- Users scroll or search for the next step
- Decision momentum is lost between intent and action
- Users click wrong elements looking for the CTA

User Impact:
Drop-off at conversion-critical moments. Users who were ready to act lose confidence or get distracted before finding the button.

Good UX Principle:
Primary call-to-action is placed immediately adjacent to the content that builds motivation and intent. The user's eye naturally flows from understanding to action without searching. CTAs are visually prominent and positioned where decision momentum peaks.

Actionable Correction:
Place CTAs directly adjacent to motivating content. Ensure visual prominence through size, color, and contrast. Position action buttons where user decision momentum is highest.
