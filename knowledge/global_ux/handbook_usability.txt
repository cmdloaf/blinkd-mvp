TITLE: Global Bad UX Knowledge Base (Handbook of Usability and User Experience)
SOURCE: Handbook of Usability and User Experience: Methods and Techniques — Soares, Rebelo, Ahram (CRC Press, 2022)

INFERENCE RULE:
When a BAD UX pattern is detected, infer a GOOD UX improvement that:
- improves task effectiveness, efficiency, and satisfaction
- aligns with user goals, experience level, and context of use
- reduces cognitive, physical, or emotional friction
- avoids visual styling prescriptions
- remains actionable and defensible

---

ID: handbook_goal_misalignment
Category: Task Fit

Description:
Systems are built around features or technology instead of what users are trying to accomplish.

Symptoms:
- Users must adapt their workflow to the system
- Core tasks require unnecessary steps
- System success metrics differ from user success

Impact:
Causes frustration, abandonment, and perceived poor usability.

---

ID: handbook_ignoring_context
Category: Context of Use

Description:
Interfaces are designed without considering where, how, and under what conditions users interact.

Symptoms:
- Same interface for all environments
- No adaptation to physical, social, or situational context
- Lab-tested flows that break in real-world use

Impact:
Usability degrades when conditions differ from design assumptions.

---

ID: handbook_assumed_expertise
Category: Onboarding

Description:
Designs presume familiarity with domain concepts, terminology, or workflows.

Symptoms:
- Technical jargon without explanation
- No onboarding or progressive disclosure
- Interfaces optimized only for expert users

Impact:
Excludes novices and increases learning friction.

---

ID: handbook_poor_feedback
Category: System Status

Description:
Users are not informed about what the system is doing or the result of their actions.

Symptoms:
- Actions without confirmation
- Delayed responses without progress indicators
- Unclear success or failure states

Impact:
Creates uncertainty and mistrust in the system.

---

ID: handbook_low_error_tolerance
Category: Error Handling

Description:
Systems fail to anticipate mistakes or help users recover from them.

Symptoms:
- Strict input validation
- Errors without guidance
- No undo or correction mechanisms

Impact:
Increases anxiety and discourages exploration.

---

ID: handbook_task_interface_mismatch
Category: Usability

Description:
The interface structure does not match how users think about or perform tasks.

Symptoms:
- Functions grouped by system logic, not user logic
- Important actions hidden behind irrelevant steps
- Navigation that does not reflect user mental models

Impact:
Slows task completion and increases cognitive effort.

---

ID: handbook_inconsistent_behavior
Category: Usability

Description:
Similar actions behave differently across the system.

Symptoms:
- Inconsistent terminology
- Different results for similar inputs
- Changing interaction patterns without explanation

Impact:
Users must relearn behaviors and lose confidence.

---

ID: handbook_no_user_control
Category: Flow Integrity

Description:
Users are forced into rigid flows without alternatives.

Symptoms:
- No customization or shortcuts
- Mandatory linear processes
- System interruptions that block progress

Impact:
Reduces efficiency and perceived autonomy.

---

ID: handbook_poor_learnability
Category: Onboarding

Description:
Interfaces are difficult to learn or remember after time away.

Symptoms:
- Complex interactions without cues
- Overloaded screens for first-time users
- No reinforcement of learned behaviors

Impact:
Users forget how to use the system and require relearning.

---

ID: handbook_no_real_user_testing
Category: Validation

Description:
Usability decisions are made without observing real user behavior.

Symptoms:
- Assumptions replacing validation
- Metrics without behavioral grounding
- Overreliance on internal opinions

Impact:
Design decisions drift away from real user needs.
