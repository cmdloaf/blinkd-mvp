ID: expectation_alignment
Category: Usability
Description:
Interface behavior consistently matches what users expect based on visual cues, labels, and prior experience. Buttons do what their labels say, navigation goes where users anticipate, form submissions produce predictable results, and progress indicators accurately reflect progress.

Prevents:
- Expectation mismatch
- User surprise and confusion after actions
- Trust erosion from unpredictable behavior
- Excessive use of back button

Outcome:
Users develop confidence in the interface and take actions decisively. Trust builds with each predictable interaction, leading to faster task completion and higher satisfaction.
