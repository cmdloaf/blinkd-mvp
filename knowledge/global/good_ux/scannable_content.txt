ID: scannable_content
Category: Cognitive Load
Description:
Content is structured using hierarchy, spacing, bullet points, and visual chunking so users can scan and locate key information without reading every word. Key points are highlighted or separated from supporting detail. Information density is managed through progressive layers.

Prevents:
- Text density overload
- Instruction skipping
- Comprehension failure
- Cognitive fatigue during task completion

Outcome:
Users find critical information quickly. Reduced cognitive fatigue and higher task completion confidence. Users feel in control of the information rather than overwhelmed by it.
