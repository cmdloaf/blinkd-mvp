ID: uninterrupted_workflow
Category: Flow Integrity
Description:
Task flows are designed to be completed without forced detours, popups, or context switches. Auxiliary tasks (like account settings or optional upgrades) are accessible but never injected into the critical path. The user maintains focus and momentum from start to finish.

Prevents:
- Workflow interruption
- Loss of flow state
- Task abandonment due to distraction
- User frustration from forced context switches

Outcome:
Users complete tasks efficiently with sustained focus. The product feels respectful of the user's time and attention. Task completion rates and satisfaction scores improve.
