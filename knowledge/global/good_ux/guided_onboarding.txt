ID: guided_onboarding
Category: Onboarding
Description:
New users are introduced to the product through a structured, goal-oriented sequence that connects features to user value. The onboarding flow shows what the product does, helps users complete a meaningful first action, and builds confidence through early wins. Guidance is contextual and disappears once the user demonstrates competence.

Prevents:
- Onboarding failure
- First-session abandonment
- Feature misunderstanding
- Blank-slate paralysis

Outcome:
Users reach the product's value proposition quickly and form a positive first impression. Activation rates improve because users understand how the product serves their goals.
