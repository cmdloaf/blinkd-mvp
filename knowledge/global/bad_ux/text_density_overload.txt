ID: text_density_overload
Category: Cognitive Load
Description:
Large blocks of unbroken text presented during task-critical moments. Users face walls of content without visual hierarchy, spacing, or chunking to aid scanning. Paragraphs run together without clear separation of key points from supporting detail.

Symptoms:
- Users hesitate before reading
- Users skip instructions entirely
- Users miss critical information buried in paragraphs
- Increased time-on-page without corresponding comprehension
- Users re-read sections multiple times

Impact:
Confusion, abandonment, and failure to complete intended actions. Users who do read everything experience cognitive fatigue and reduced confidence in their decisions.

Opposite (Good UX Principle):
scannable_content
