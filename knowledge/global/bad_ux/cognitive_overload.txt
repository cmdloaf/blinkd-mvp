ID: cognitive_overload
Category: Cognitive Load
Description:
Too many choices, inputs, or information presented simultaneously without progressive revelation. Users are asked to process multiple concepts, make multiple decisions, or provide extensive input all at once. Forms with many fields, dashboards with excessive data points, or onboarding flows that explain everything upfront.

Symptoms:
- Users freeze or hesitate when faced with too many options
- Users abandon forms midway through
- Users make errors due to attention splitting
- Users feel overwhelmed and anxious
- Decision paralysis leads to inaction

Impact:
Task abandonment, increased error rates, and negative emotional associations with the product. Users may not return after experiencing overwhelm.

Opposite (Good UX Principle):
progressive_disclosure
